# User Personas

## 1. Lakshmi
*   **Demographics:** 55 years old, rural farmer's wife, living in a village near Madurai.
*   **Language:** Tamil only.
*   **Digital Literacy:** Very low. Cannot read complex UI, relies on voice assistants.
*   **Goals:** Find financial assistance schemes for agriculture, widow pension (if applicable), or women's support.
*   **Pain Points:** Typing is impossible. Official jargon is confusing. Navigating complex websites is impossible.
*   **Technology Comfort:** Only comfortable tapping a microphone button and speaking.
*   **Typical Queries:** "விவசாயிகளுக்கு என்ன உதவி இருக்கு?" (What help is there for farmers?), "மகளிர் உரிமை தொகை எப்படி வாங்குவது?" (How to get women's right money?)
*   **Relevant Schemes:** Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai, Uzhavar Pathukappu Thittam.

## 2. Karthik
*   **Demographics:** 22 years old, unemployed recent graduate, living in Coimbatore.
*   **Language:** Tanglish.
*   **Digital Literacy:** High.
*   **Goals:** Find skill development programs, unemployment allowance, or startup loans.
*   **Pain Points:** Information is scattered. Hard to know exact eligibility criteria.
*   **Technology Comfort:** Expert. Prefers typing fast in Tanglish or English.
*   **Typical Queries:** "bro unemployment scheme edhavadhu irukka?", "Na bsc cs mudichuruken, skill training schemes in tn"
*   **Relevant Schemes:** Naan Mudhalvan, Unemployed Youth Employment Generation Programme (UYEGP).

## 3. Meena
*   **Demographics:** 35 years old, urban working woman, Chennai.
*   **Language:** Tamil + English.
*   **Digital Literacy:** Medium to High.
*   **Goals:** Health insurance for her aging parents, educational schemes for her children.
*   **Pain Points:** Limited time. Wants quick, precise answers and list of required documents without wading through PDFs.
*   **Technology Comfort:** Uses smartphones daily, comfortable with both text and voice.
*   **Typical Queries:** "Chief minister health insurance apply panna enna documents venum?", "Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar scheme eligibility for my daughter"
*   **Relevant Schemes:** CMCHIS (Health Insurance), Pudhumai Penn Scheme.

## 4. Rajan
*   **Demographics:** 62 years old, retired fisherman, Nagapattinam.
*   **Language:** Tamil only.
*   **Digital Literacy:** Low.
*   **Goals:** Old age pension, fisherman welfare board benefits.
*   **Pain Points:** Eyesight issues (needs large text or audio output), afraid of making mistakes in applications.
*   **Technology Comfort:** Has a smartphone but mainly uses WhatsApp. Needs voice output to understand the response.
*   **Typical Queries:** "முதியோர் உதவித்தொகைக்கு எங்கு போக வேண்டும்?" (Where should I go for old age pension?)
*   **Relevant Schemes:** OAP (Old Age Pension), Fishermen Welfare schemes.

## 6. Selvam
*   **Demographics:** 40 years old, daily wage laborer with a physical disability, Tirunelveli.
*   **Language:** Tamil.
*   **Digital Literacy:** Very low.
*   **Goals:** Disability pension, free bus pass, assistance for assistive devices.
*   **Pain Points:** Cannot travel to government offices just to ask questions. Needs accurate offline-like help.
*   **Technology Comfort:** Very limited. Assisted by his son sometimes, but wants to use voice on his own.
*   **Typical Queries:** "மாற்றுத்திறனாளிகளுக்கு இலவச பஸ் பாஸ் வாங்குவது எப்படி?" (How to get free bus pass for disabled?)
*   **Relevant Schemes:** Differently Abled Pension, Free Bus Pass scheme.
